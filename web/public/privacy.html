Privacy and bank-data notice

Effective August 16, 2026. Household CFO Method uses Plaid only when you choose to connect a financial institution.

What we retrieve

We retrieve account names, partial account numbers, balances, and transaction history needed to help you review spending and maintain your household plan. We do not receive or store your bank username or password, request payment access, initiate payments, or move money.

How decisions work

Authorized bank activity is available in the app and to Mia so you can search, summarize, and review it. Pending charges and money coming in remain informational. Posted expenses are prepared automatically as review drafts, but they do not change categorized budget actuals until you confirm them. Optional trusted-merchant automation is off by default and can confirm only after the same merchant and category have been approved repeatedly within strict amount and duplicate checks.

Storage and service providers

Plaid access credentials are encrypted and kept only on the server. We retain normalized account and transaction fields needed to operate the product, not raw Plaid API payloads. When you ask Mia about bank activity, a limited verified summary—such as merchant, date, amount, review state, or aggregate totals—may be sent to our configured AI narration provider to compose the response. Plaid credentials, bank-login credentials, raw Plaid responses, and full account identifiers are not sent to the AI provider. Analytics excludes financial values and masks session text and inputs. We use service providers only to operate and secure the application and do not sell bank data.

Your choices

You can disconnect an institution at any time. Disconnecting asks Plaid to remove the Item, removes the stored access credential and unapproved Plaid source data, and keeps financial records you already approved.

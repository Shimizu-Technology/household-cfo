Privacy and bank data notice

Effective August 16, 2026. Your program may offer a Plaid bank connection only when you choose to authorize it.

Secure sign-in

The workspace uses WorkOS for secure sign-in. If you choose Google, Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with WorkOS to authenticate you. The workspace uses your verified sign-in identity to open your invited account and keep access linked to your household records. Google sign-in does not grant access to Gmail, Google Drive files, or payment services. Only approved program accounts can open a workspace.

What the workspace retrieves

The workspace retrieves account names, partial account numbers, balances, and transaction history needed to help you review spending and maintain your plan. It does not receive or store your bank username or password, request payment access, initiate payments, or move money.

How decisions work

Authorized bank activity is available in the workspace and to your configured assistant so you can search, summarize, and review it. Pending charges and money coming in remain informational. Posted expenses can be prepared as review drafts, but they do not change categorized budget actuals until you confirm them.

Storage and service providers

Plaid access credentials are encrypted and kept only on the server. The service retains normalized account and transaction fields needed to operate the workspace, not raw Plaid API payloads. When you ask your assistant about bank activity, a limited verified summary may be sent to the configured AI narration provider to compose the response. Plaid credentials, bank login credentials, raw Plaid responses, and full account identifiers are not sent to the AI provider. Analytics excludes financial values and masks session text and inputs. Bank data is not sold.

Your choices

You can disconnect an institution at any time. Disconnecting asks Plaid to remove the Item, removes the stored access credential and unapproved Plaid source data, and keeps financial records you already approved.

Contact

Use the support link in your program workspace for questions or deletion requests.

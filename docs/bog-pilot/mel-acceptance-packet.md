## Short synthetic walkthrough with Mel

Use fictional data and record the tested release/version. This walkthrough is still to be performed with Mel; local engineering demonstrations are not her acceptance.

1. Enroll without debt or a full annual budget. Review the dates, voluntary target and privacy notice. Choose $300 for the demonstration, then separately inspect the postponed-target experience.
2. Review a three-month baseline and an incomplete baseline. Explain the coverage labels, correct a mistaken classification and choose a comfortable experiment without treating essentials as spare money.
3. Stage and explicitly approve $100 of eligible new reserve money. Link $25.50 of reviewed evidence, then approve a $25 withdrawal. The result is $75 reported and $0.50 supported, included within $75. Pending changes must not replace approved progress.
4. Record a purchase with optional feelings. Correct or void a participant-owned purchase, demonstrate the no-spend conflict, then erase feelings and confirm the financial history stays intact. A source-owned purchase must follow source correction rules.
5. Inspect a fictional optional card with a $300 known balance and unknown APR, minimum and due date. Unknown must stay unknown. The app must not invent an avalanche order, extra payment, surplus or payoff promise.
6. Ask Mia about optional cards/full budgeting, the $75/$300/$0.50 figures, essentials and loan guarantees, and employer access. Decline sharing, grant narrowly if desired, revoke it and inspect the coach view. Export personal records without optional feelings. Demonstrate frozen checkpoints and explicit later revisions using a test clock, not by advancing a real participant's calendar.

# Own-account transfers and person payments (B7)

Context: Household CFO participants upload statements, review account ownership, then approve source lines. Budget projects categorized approved purchases; Mia reads approved source heads. Neither should count an own-account transfer as spending or income. Sources: `ParticipantPlan`, `LineApprover`, `EconomicLinker`, `ApprovedSourceReader`, `SpendingPatterns::Analyzer`, and the fictional Talun manifest. Implementation was rebased onto `4ec5dbc5354918cc77d7c96c64d4d758d4289388` on November 20, 2026, including B6 refunds (#334), upload-status handling (#335) and Apple Cash parsing (#336).

Agent-executed affected-flow acceptance:

1. Upload checking and savings CSVs; review both account identities. Equal opposing transfer movements within three days on distinct reviewed accounts offer a pair. Approving the pair saves both sides as movements and creates no spending projection. Repeating the save changes nothing.
2. Unpair those lines. Both remain reviewed transfers, excluded from spending and income. The app does not immediately offer the rejected pair again. Changing a line later uses its existing review form.
3. An unpaired outgoing Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, or person-payment line asks whose account it is. Until answered, it stays in attention. Own account saves a transfer; someone else saves categorized family-support spending. Mia never offers this support as a spending cut, even if its display name is later corrected.
4. Refuse ambiguous equal matches, same-account matches, stale account/line identities, pending corrections, other households, old financial pictures and old extraction revisions.
5. Repeat visible interactions at desktop 1366×900, phone 390×844 and compact phone 320px in Chromium. Verify saved Budget/Mia output through the local API. Screenshots are outside this public checkout in `/Users/agent/work/hcfo-evidence/fix-transfer-pairing/`.
6. Replay full and fast scorecards offline, both approval views; own-transfer spending/income leakage stays zero. Regenerate baselines with Runner, never by editing metrics. Run the full Rails/web gates and affected browser regressions.

Production data: there is no migration or deployment-time rewrite. Existing approved source heads and their projections continue to count exactly as approved. Pair suggestions apply to unapproved lines and may reference an already-approved transfer counterpart without changing that counterpart. Unpairing is an explicit participant action that appends review versions; it does not turn either movement into spending or income. A previously approved person payment is not reclassified on deploy.

No live model or paid service is used. Native CSV extraction and fictional recorded scorecard responses are the only readers used for acceptance. WebKit cannot run on this Mac mini; Chromium resizing does not establish Safari or physical-phone acceptance.
